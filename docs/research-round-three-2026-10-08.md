# Audience and niche articles: research and editorial decisions

Six new local drafts were researched and written on 8 October 2026. They extend the existing 113-article library to 119 articles across 130 pages. Nothing was published, submitted for indexing or changed on the hosted website.

The articles are AI-assisted editorial work. Primary references support specific platform or policy facts, while the planning frameworks, worksheets and fictional examples are editorial analysis. No expert review, real customer outcome, product test, property verification or campaign performance is claimed. The source-check date and method appear visibly in each new article.

## Why these six topics

The existing library already covers general YouTube clipping, SaaS founders, webinars, D2C campaigns, rights and disclosure. Repeating those subjects with slightly different titles would add little value. These articles instead answer narrower decisions for the audiences the owner requested.

| Audience | New article | Specific contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Educational YouTubers | YouTube tutorial clipping | A complete learner-task approach, lesson matrix, screen/caption checks, related-video constraints and a fictional teaching storyboard |
| Company owners and B2B teams | B2B webinar clipping | Buying-role selection, claim evidence, question-matched destinations, lead qualification and a sales handoff |
| Influencers and advertisers | Sponsored-content clipping | Reuse-scope questions, disclosure after recutting, truthful creator voice, an approval register and a fictional sponsored-bag storyboard |
| Restaurant and café owners | Restaurant/café clipping | Branch-specific menu and visit information, filming evidence, expiring offers, booking handoff and an attendance calculation |
| Property marketing teams | Real estate walkthrough clipping | Actual-unit/model/render distinctions, dated property facts, spatial continuity, applicable advertising review and visit reporting |
| Clothing brands and fashion creators | Fashion product-demo clipping | Size/measurement distinctions, actual variant evidence, movement and detail shots, matching destinations and retained-order reporting |

The proposed query phrases are stored in `src/editorial/researched/round-three/plan.cjs`. They map editorial intent; they are not newly measured search-volume estimates or proof of GSC demand. The owner’s earlier snapshot supplies broad clipping context. No fresh GSC account data was obtained for this batch.

## Search guidance used

[Google’s people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) informed the emphasis on original contribution, a clear audience, useful depth and honest authorship. Article length is a coverage observation, not a ranking formula. Its page showed an update date of 5 October 2026 when checked.

[Google’s AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) says there is no special optimization or additional schema required for its AI features. The implementation therefore uses crawlable article text, clear answers, relevant internal links and accurate existing Article/Breadcrumb metadata. Eligibility does not guarantee inclusion, ranking or citations.

No competitor keyword variations were inserted merely to attract unrelated navigation queries. No new blanket FAQPage/HowTo markup, fabricated author credentials or unsupported client proof was added.

## Practical contribution and implementation

The six drafts add 84 individually authored sections, 45 FAQs and 16 tables. Their 15,932 reading words include direct answers, headings, paragraphs, lists, table cells and FAQs. Each article is approximately 2,500–2,800 words under that method. All numerical campaign examples are explicitly hypothetical; their denominators and attribution limits are explained.

Eighteen contextual links from existing articles connect readers to the new subjects. The latest six appear first in the main resource index and at the top of their Learn/Journal collections. All are included in native library filtering, the lazily loaded search index, related reading and the sitemap. Body content and native FAQs remain available without JavaScript.

New drafts use `dateCreated` and `dateModified` of 8 October 2026, with matching visible dates. They do not claim an earlier publication date. Existing article prose is preserved; new contextual links are appended to the relevant established articles. The sitemap records current changes to affected routes and collection indexes.

The shared CSS, scripts, neutral themes, corrected artwork and transparent footer logo are unchanged. The larger resource/search indexes are the expected content cost; no new runtime feature or third-party script is required for this batch.
