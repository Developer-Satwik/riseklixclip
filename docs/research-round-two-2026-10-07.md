Searches were discovery research, not a country-controlled ranking study. The earlier GSC connector check in this session was blocked by its subscription status. This round uses the supplied query evidence and current primary documentation; it does not claim a fresh GSC export or a new AI-citation dataset. Relevant supplied themes include Indian clipping campaigns/platforms, marketplaces, becoming a clipper, clipping jobs, streamer campaigns, gaming, creator distribution and campaign legitimacy. Some new resources answer adjacent operating questions rather than exact-match queries observed in that snapshot.
