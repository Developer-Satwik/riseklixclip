# RiseKlix language and wording review

Reviewed and implemented on 8 October 2026. The brief is to feel polished and capable while remaining easy to understand for Indian creators, company owners, clients and clippers. Premium presentation does not imply higher prices, exclusivity or a minimum budget.

## Voice to use

Write as a capable person explaining the next step to another person. Be clear, respectful and specific. Let confidence come from knowing the work: the content needed, the review process and the decisions to agree. Keep a little character in campaign headlines, then explain the service in ordinary language.

Use familiar verbs: choose, make, share, check, agree, ask, review and send. Prefer a complete short explanation over an impressive label. Keep the language consistent across light and dark themes, mobile and desktop, and scripted and native form responses.

Do not imply that clients must already know campaign terminology. A rough idea is enough to start an enquiry. Budget ranges are planning information, not package prices. Do not invent prices, availability, income, results or response-time promises.

Avoid vague agency phrases such as “creator squad”, “clipping atelier” and “field library” in navigation or instructions. Avoid claims such as “unleash growth”, “viral on demand”, “India’s best”, “elite clients” or “affordable for everyone” without relevant evidence. Do not replace these with an AI-centric service description.

Do not force Hinglish, slang or a Hindi translation to signal an Indian identity. Match the language to the actual source and intended audience in a campaign. Preserve regional names and review captions with someone who understands the language.

## Applied examples

| Previous wording | Current wording | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explore / Learn / Journal | Resources / Guides / Articles | Let visitors recognise the destination. URLs stay the same. |
| The clipping atelier | Clip by RiseKlix | Use the actual brand name in the hero caption. |
| A brief, a creator squad and a review process | The content, brief and editing process | Explain the work without introducing an unfamiliar team label. |
| The field library | Resource library | Make the article collection easy to identify. |
| Your first step. Join the Discord. | Want to clip? Start on Discord. | State who the section is for and the next action. |
| Help me scope it | Not sure — let’s discuss it | Make the optional budget choice conversational. The internal form value stays compatible with the server. |
| Gross reward scenario | Estimated payment before fees | Explain what the calculator number represents. |
| Browser storage is unavailable | Your browser could not keep it for later | Explain the practical consequence when saved reading is only available for this visit. |
| The direct answer | Quick answer | A familiar label for an article’s opening explanation. |

Keep precise terms where removing them would change meaning. “Eligible views” are not all views. “Attribution” is a connection made by a measurement method, not proof of causation. “Approval” and “payment” are different states. The article glossaries explain these distinctions using native HTML disclosures, without loading another script.

## Status and error copy

Tell people what happened, what remains available and what they can do next. Keep required and optional fields visibly identified. Retain answers after a failed enquiry attempt and offer copy/email recovery. Copying a brief or enquiry must not be described as sending it.

An accepted email request is not confirmed inbox delivery. The scripted and native receipt both explain that distinction without promising a campaign booking. Private data should not be included in public source links or community channels. The enquiry test uses mocked provider responses and sends no real email.

## Review after release

Use actual Search Console page/query data to watch relevant discovery and use privacy-respecting interaction data to examine enquiry completion and Discord entry. Compare over a meaningful period rather than assigning any movement to wording alone. A future comprehension check should include an Indian creator, a company owner and a beginner clipper: ask each to explain the service, identify the next action and describe how payment is confirmed. No such user study is claimed here.
