Start with the campaign scope

Define the audience, intended language, source content and goal. Agree whether the deliverables are published clips, files for owned channels or both.

Use the editable campaign brief to record the plan before recruiting editors.

Join the community

The RiseKlix Discord community is the starting point for questions and campaign enquiries. Check current availability and eligibility with the operator; joining a server does not establish a paid role.

Open the RiseKlix Discord community ↗

Read the current brief

Find the approved source library, permitted platforms, creative boundaries, required disclosure, deadline, submission fields and review criteria. Ask about anything unclear before starting work.

Keep sources and rights together

Use material approved for the campaign. Keep the source link, relevant timestamp and context with the edit. Check restrictions on music, third-party footage, private information and commercial reuse.

Submit a complete record

Include the campaign name, posting account or Discord handle, platform, public link or file, source timestamp, caption, disclosure status and upload time. Send private payout information only through the agreed private workflow.

Review and revise

The reviewer checks the edit against the brief, source context, quality, rights and platform requirements. Keep the approval decision and specific feedback with the submission. Agree how disputes or unavailable links will be handled.

Verify the available evidence

Use public links and timestamped metric snapshots. Authorised platform insights can add context when access is available. Record unavailable information as unavailable; a screenshot alone does not establish unique viewers, organic traffic or sales.

Account connections should use the platform’s authorised connection flow. Never send a social account password to join a campaign.

Agree payment conditions

Confirm the rate, budget cap, eligible metric, fees, review window and payment schedule in writing. Keep pending review, approved, scheduled and paid amounts separate. A reward estimate is not a payment confirmation.

Close the campaign with learnings

Collect approved links, metric timestamps, spending, rejection reasons and creative observations. Distinguish observed results from estimates. Decide what the next brief should keep or change.

Read the privacy policy, terms and deletion process before participating in tracked campaign work.

Plan before joining a campaign

Confirm current availability, source permissions, eligibility, review criteria and payment terms. Keep the approved brief and submission records available for review.
