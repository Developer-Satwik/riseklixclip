05Common questions

Before the
first cut.

Answers to help you get started.

Talk to the team ↗
What is an Indian clipping campaign?＋

An Indian clipping campaign brings people together to turn approved videos into short clips for an Indian audience. The brief sets the content, language, platforms, review and payment rules.

Does clipping work in India?＋

Clipping can help you reuse good content for a relevant audience. Start with a small batch, check the response and decide whether to continue. Views, income and sales are not guaranteed.

Is RiseKlix a clipping platform or a managed network?＋

Clip by RiseKlix is a managed clipping network, with its community on Discord. Talk to the team about the work you need and current availability. You can also use the guides, comparisons and planning tools on this website.

How do I become a clipper?＋

Learn the basics and make samples using content you have permission to use. Join our Discord community, follow the welcome instructions and ask about current campaigns. Confirm the brief and payment terms before working. Joining does not guarantee paid work.

Can Indian clippers receive UPI payouts?＋

UPI may be available if the campaign team supports it. Confirm the rate, what counts for payment, any fees and the payment schedule before editing.
