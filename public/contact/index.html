    What happens next / 02

    From idea
    to a clear plan.

    1. We review your enquiry.

      Your content, audience, and campaign goal give the conversation a starting point.

    2. We discuss the scope.

      Discuss which videos can be used, the formats, platforms, reviews and budget.

    3. You decide the next step.

      Confirm availability and agree the work and payment terms in writing before starting.
